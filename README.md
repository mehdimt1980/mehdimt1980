## 🧠 About Me

I'm an **AI Product Builder** and **Co-Founder** based in **Munich**, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, healthcare operations, and research.

My current work focuses on designing **agentic AI systems** that support complex planning, resource allocation, decision-making, and organizational coordination in clinical environments.

- 🏥 Building **intelligent OR staff-planning engines** at [ORaigent](https://oraigent.com)
- 🤖 Creating **agentic AI solutions** at [Nexus Agent AI](https://www.nexusagentai.com)
- 📚 Researching **NLP & Digital Humanities** with computational text analysis
- 🔐 Developing **privacy-first AI tools** for healthcare data
